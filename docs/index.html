TEAM 52

AgentGate

A real-time firewall for AI agent tool calls

What the demo shows

  • A normal $20 refund is allowed.
  • A poisoned customer ticket leaks customer data when the gate is off, and is blocked when the gate is on, with the policies it breaks cited.
  • A $600 refund is paused until a person approves it.
  • Every decision is saved in an audit log.
  • A live Claude worker agent handles a ticket with every tool call checked.
  • Benchmark: 30 attacks. No defense let all 30 through, a keyword filter 20, AgentGate 0.

Every decision in the video is a real, live Claude call. The tools are simulated, so nothing real is sent.
